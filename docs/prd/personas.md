## Primary User — The Gym Beginner

- **Context**: Just started gym, following a fixed routine
- **Need**: Log workouts quickly without thinking too much
- **Pain**: Existing apps are bloated with features they don't need
- **Device**: Phone (primary) or desktop
- **Frequency**: 5 days/week

## Family Group

- **Context**: Siblings starting gym together
- **Need**: See each other's progress for motivation
- **Pain**: No easy way to compare without sharing phones
- **Privacy**: Stats are public within family, photos are private
